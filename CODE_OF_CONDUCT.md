# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Euboulia community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
  and
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct and will give a
reason for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents
the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through
the repository's maintainer contact or private reporting channel. All complaints
will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and
security of reporters and affected people as far as reasonably possible.

Responses may progress from a private correction, to a warning, temporary
restriction, or permanent removal, depending on impact, intent, recurrence, and
the safety of the community. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a
violation.
